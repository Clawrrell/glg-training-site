Verify Your Own Work Before Anyone Else Sees It

The best quality control is the one you run yourself. Every time. Before every handoff.

🔍 The Pre-Handoff Audit Checklist

Before sending any handoff email, run through this checklist. If ANY item fails, fix it before proceeding.

Audit ItemStandard
CasePeer Missing Data ReportRun filtered to this case — ZERO results required
Client informationName, DOB, SSN (last 4), address, phone, email, employer, language preference, referral source — all populated
Incident detailsDate, time, location, description, police report #
Insurance — ALL fieldsAt-fault carrier + policy #, UM/UIM status + limits, health insurance. No blank fields. "Unknown" with follow-up notation is acceptable; blank is not.
Medical informationInjuries, ER visit, treating providers, prior injuries to same body parts
Documents — dual-uploadRetainer, HIPAA, police report — in BOTH OneDrive AND CasePeer
SOL calendaredSame day as case acceptance, with named owner
SB 68 (post-April 2025 MVAs)Seatbelt status documented verbatim
Red flags notedGovernment entity, commercial vehicle, minor, prior representation — all flagged
Case grading inputs preparedInjury severity, liability, policy limits, estimated value — ready for grade assignment
CasePeer statusSet to "Intake" (will change to "Intake Review" at handoff)
The Missing Data Report Is Your Gate: In CasePeer, go to Reports → Missing Data → filter to this case. If ANY results come back, your file is not ready for handoff. Fix the gaps first. This is the single most important habit you can build: after every intake, before every handoff, run the Missing Data report. Zero results = green light. Any results = stop and fix.

🔁 Build the Habit: After CasePeer Entry → Run Audit Checklist

Make the self-audit automatic. Every time you finish entering a case in CasePeer, run the Missing Data report as your very next action. Not after lunch. Not before the handoff email. Immediately after entry. The audit is fastest when the case is still fresh in your mind.

The Dual-Upload Rule (From IR-I-2): Every document must be uploaded to BOTH OneDrive AND CasePeer. OneDrive is the file storage system. CasePeer is the case management system. If a document is only in one location, it's incomplete. The CM needs both — OneDrive for the actual file, CasePeer for the data and tracking. Single-upload = compliance failure.

Section 5 · Post-Handoff Ownership · Up to 40 pts

Your Job Doesn't End When You Hit "Send"

The handoff email is the beginning of the transition — not the end of your responsibility.
Real-World Consequence

A client signed their retainer on Monday. The handoff email went out Tuesday. Nobody followed up on the unsigned HIPAA authorization. Six weeks later, the CM is trying to request medical records — but there's no signed authorization on file. The CM asks Priya: "Was the HIPAA ever signed?" Priya checks: no, it wasn't. The client fell through the cracks. Six weeks of case progression lost.

Priya assumed the handoff transferred ALL responsibility. But per the Intake SOP, unsigned document follow-up remains Intake's responsibility — not the CM's. The follow-up cadence is specific: 48 hours → 5 business days → 10 business days (escalate). Priya owned this task until it was done. The case stalled because nobody was watching.

📋 Post-Handoff Tasks That Remain Yours

  • CM acknowledgment follow-up: Verify the assigned CM acknowledges the case within 24 hours of handoff. If no acknowledgment → escalate to Adriauna (Head Pre-Lit Attorney).
  • Unsigned document cadence: 48 hours (first follow-up) → 5 business days (second follow-up) → 10 business days (escalate to Managing Attorney). This is YOUR cadence, not the CM's.
  • Fix any post-handoff deficiencies: If the CM flags a missing field or document, fix it immediately. The error is yours, not theirs.
Ownership Doesn't Transfer at "Send": Sending the handoff email does not absolve you of open items. If a document is unsigned, YOU follow up. If the CM doesn't acknowledge, YOU escalate. If a field was blank that you missed, YOU fix it. The handoff is confirmed when the CM has a complete file AND acknowledges receipt — not when you press send.

Section 6 · Synthesis & Integration · Up to 40 pts

Putting It All Together

The complete picture: audit, handoff, follow-up, and downstream impact — connected.

🔗 The Complete Intake Quality Cycle

  1. Execute: Conduct intake per IR-I-1 (screening), IR-I-2 (interview/CasePeer), IR-I-3 (onboarding)
  2. Audit: Run Missing Data report → verify dual-upload → confirm SOL → check SB 68 → validate all fields
  3. Grade: Prepare grading inputs → assign grade → notify Adriauna same-day for Grade A
  4. Hand Off: Send email (To/CC per pattern) → change CasePeer status to "Intake Review" → within 3 business days of retainer
  5. Follow Up: Verify CM acknowledgment (24h) → unsigned doc cadence (48h/5d/10d) → fix any post-handoff deficiencies
  6. Confirm: Handoff is COMPLETE when CM has acknowledged, all documents are signed, and zero callbacks have occurred
What the CM Needs From You: When a CM prepares for the welcome call, they review: client name and injuries, treatment status, last communication date, CasePeer notes, insurance coverage, and next steps. If ANY of these are missing from your file, the CM walks into the call unprepared — and the client notices.
