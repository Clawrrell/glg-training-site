Inline Primer — What is a "Drop" vs. a "Decline"? A drop ends an existing representation — the attorney-client relationship has already formed, and GLG is withdrawing. They are never interchangeable.

Section 2 · Drop Triggers & the 14-Day Hold · Up to 40 pts

When Cases Get Dropped — and What Happens First

No case drops itself. Every drop requires attorney authorization, a documented hold period, and a process. Skipping any step is an ethics exposure.

🚨 The Four Drop Triggers — Each Requires Attorney Authorization

TriggerDefinitionFirst CM Action
Non-complianceClient fails to follow through on treatment, attend appointments, respond to outreach, or cooperate with the case process over an extended periodDocument all outreach attempts, escalate to attorney
Liability issuesRe-investigation reveals new facts that significantly undermine the case (shared fault, credibility problems, no provable negligence)Attorney reviews and makes the drop call — CM escalates
Coverage exhaustionAvailable insurance coverage is insufficient to cover documented damages AND no other coverage sources existVerify all coverage sources before escalating to attorney
Client requestThe client affirmatively asks to end the representationDo not process independently — transfer to attorney immediately

⏳ The 14-Day Hold Period — Required Before Every Drop

Once the attorney authorizes a drop, CasePeer moves to Pending Drop — not Dropped. The 14-day hold begins. During this period:

  1. At least one documented outreach attempt must be made, communicating clearly why the case is at risk and what the client can do to re-engage
  2. If the client responds and addresses the issue, the attorney evaluates whether to proceed with or cancel the drop
  3. If the hold expires with no response, the attorney confirms: proceed with drop → Template #29 goes out
  4. CasePeer stays in Pending Drop until the hold period is formally resolved — it cannot skip to Dropped
The Hold Period Protects GLG. The 14 days creates a documented record that the client was given a final, reasonable opportunity to respond before withdrawal. Without this record, GLG cannot demonstrate it met its professional obligations under the Rules of Professional Conduct. No shortcuts.
Verbal Policy = No Policy. Attorney verbal approval for a drop is the start of the process — not the end of it. Until CasePeer shows Pending Drop and the hold is logged, the drop has not been initiated from a documentation standpoint.

Section 3 · Templates #29 & #30 · Up to 40 pts

Drop Letter vs. Declined Case Letter — and Client Communication Rules

Two templates. Completely different situations. Using the wrong one is a documentation error with potential ethics consequences.

📄 Template Reference — Drop & Decline Documents

TemplateNameWhen UsedRelationship Status
#29Case_Drop_LetterAfter the 14-day hold expires with no client response; formal notice of withdrawal from existing representationExisting client — attorney-client relationship existed
#30Declined_Case_LetterAt intake, when GLG declines to represent a prospective client; no representation ever beganProspective client only — no attorney-client relationship formed

📞 Client Communication Requirements During the 14-Day Hold

The hold period is not a communication blackout. During Pending Drop status, the CM must:

  • Make at least one documented outreach attempt — phone call, text, or email — during the hold period
  • Communicate the reason the case is at risk clearly: "Your case is in jeopardy of being closed due to [missed appointments / non-response / etc.]. To keep your case open, please contact us by [date]."
  • Log the attempt in CasePeer with the exact date, method, and outcome (no answer, left voicemail, etc.)
  • Document the client's response — or documented non-response — before the hold expires
Inline Primer — Why Timing Matters for #29. Template #29 cannot go out during the hold period. It goes out AFTER the hold expires with no client re-engagement. Sending it too early eliminates the legal benefit of the hold — the hold only protects GLG if the client had a genuine, documented opportunity to respond.
Declined Cases at Intake Need #30 Too. Even when no representation was formed, the prospective client needs written confirmation that GLG is not representing them. Without Template #30, a person who consulted with GLG might reasonably believe they have an attorney — and miss the SOL waiting for advice that was never coming.

Section 4 · CasePeer Status & Documentation · Up to 40 pts

The Four-Stage Drop Flow and Audit Trail Requirements

The status chain is non-negotiable. Every skipped step is a missing link in the ethics record — and the audit trail is what stands between GLG and a bar complaint.

🔄 CasePeer Status Flow — Case Drop Sequence

StatusWhat It MeansWhat Happens at This Stage
ActiveNormal working statusRegular CM work; attorney reviews file; if drop is authorized → move to Pending Drop
Pending Drop14-day hold in progressDocument outreach attempts; allow client to respond; DO NOT generate Template #29 yet
DroppedRepresentation formally endedTemplate #29 sent and documented; attorney-client relationship terminated; financial closeout begins
ClosedFile completeTrust reconciled + client balance returned + lien notification documented. ALL three complete before Closed.

📋 Required Drop Documentation — 4 Elements in Every File

  1. Reason for drop — specific, not generic. Not "non-compliance" but "client missed 11 scheduled appointments over 16 weeks and did not respond to 8 documented contact attempts between [dates]."
  2. Attorney who authorized — name, not just "attorney approved." Which attorney gave the drop authorization and when?
  3. Date authorization was given — starts the clock for all subsequent steps
  4. Complete outreach log during the hold period — date, method, and outcome of every contact attempt made while the file was in Pending Drop status
Dropped ≠ Closed. "Dropped" means the attorney-client relationship has ended. "Closed" certifies that the financial obligations are also resolved. A case is never moved to Closed while trust funds remain, lien notifications haven't been sent, or financial reconciliation is incomplete.

Section 5 · Ethical Obligations & Financial Closeout · Up to 40 pts

Withdrawal Rules, File Transfer Rights & Trust Account Obligations

A dropped case still has obligations. The representation ends. The duty to the client doesn't disappear the moment the letter goes out.

⚖️ Ethical Obligations on Withdrawal — Three Required Steps

Under the Rules of Professional Conduct, when a law firm withdraws from representation it must:

  1. Give reasonable notice to the client — the 14-day hold period plus Template #29 satisfies this. Notice must be given in time for the client to find new counsel without harm to their case.
  2. Take steps to protect the client's interests during transition — this includes: informing the client of any upcoming deadlines (SOL, hearings), advising them to seek new counsel promptly, and not taking actions that prejudice the client after withdrawal.
  3. Facilitate file transfer if requested — the client has the right to their complete file. GLG must provide it upon request. GLG may retain copies but cannot withhold originals.

💰 Financial Closeout on Dropped Cases — Two Non-Negotiable Steps

ObligationWhat It RequiresTiming
Trust Account ReconciliationAny client funds held in trust (retainer for costs, advanced expenses) must be returned to the client. Trust funds are never GLG's money — they belong to the client regardless of the drop reason.Before file moves to Closed
Lien NotificationIf outstanding medical liens exist, the client must be notified in writing: lien amount, lienholder name, and that the lien is now the client's responsibility to resolve. GLG does not pay client liens from firm funds. The lien does not disappear on drop.Before file moves to Closed
Trust Funds Are Never GLG's Money. On a dropped case with a trust balance, GLG cannot: retain the funds as a fee, apply them to internal costs without client authorization, hold them pending a malpractice threat, or delay return without cause. Mishandling trust funds on a dropped case is a bar rules violation.

Section 6 · Special Situations & Intake Decline · Up to 40 pts

Legal Threats, Unresponsive Clients, Attorney-Initiated Drops & Intake Declines

Every variation from the standard process has a specific protocol. When something unusual happens during a drop — stop, escalate, document.

⚠️ Special Situation Protocols

SituationCM ActionWhy
Client threatens bar complaint or lawsuitZERO response from CM; immediate escalation to attorney; all subsequent contact through attorney onlyLegal threats change the risk profile of every communication; CM is not authorized to respond to legal threats
Unresponsive client (14-day hold expires)Template #29 via certified mail AND email; both delivery attempts documented in CasePeer with tracking numberCertified mail creates the delivery record; dual-channel protects GLG if client claims they never received notice
Attorney-initiated drop (liability or strategy)Same process applies: Pending Drop, 14-day hold, Template #29 after hold; reason documented is the liability/strategy change — no privileged strategy details disclosedHold period applies regardless of who initiated the drop
Intake decline (case rejected at intake)Template #30 sent promptly; reason for declination documented in CasePeer; no representation was ever formedWithout Template #30, the prospective client may believe GLG is representing them and miss deadlines
Legal Threat = CM Silence. Any communication the CM sends after a client threatens a bar complaint or lawsuit can be used against GLG. Even a conciliatory response — "I understand your frustration" — is inadvisable without attorney review. Forward the communication. Stop responding. Document everything.
Intake Decline — Common Reasons Requiring Template #30: Statute of limitations already expired · Case type GLG does not handle · Conflict of interest discovered · Insufficient liability · Government immunity issues (GTCA applies) · Case already represented by another attorney.
